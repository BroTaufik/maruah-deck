NICTSeD 2026 · CyberSAFE Challenge Trophy · Grand Final

AI NATION 2030

Strategically transforming Malaysia into an AI-driven future while upholding ethical governance and human dignity

Our answer has one word: MARUAH. Six mechanisms. Each with an owner, a date and a metric.

MARUAH
SMK St Bernadette's Convent, Perak
THE OPENING NUMBER

Second in the world

Malaysia wants to be in the global top ten for AI by 2030. We are already number two in the world at one AI outcome: being defrauded by it.

THE QUESTION

The whole question hides in one word

The title makes three demands. The word that decides the winner is not and. It is WHILE. Both must happen at the same time, in the same country, under the same budget, inside the same sixty months.

THE PLAN IS REAL

AI Nation 2030 is a live government document

Launched by the Prime Minister on 28 July 2026 with AI Malaysia Berhad. We are not here to propose a vision. The vision exists. We are here to argue about what the plan does not yet enforce.

THE GROWTH SIDE

What the plan commits to

Every growth promise is binding, dated and measured.

THE DIGNITY SIDE

What the plan does not bind

Malaysia's dignity commitments live in the National Guidelines on AI Governance and Ethics (AIGE), issued by MOSTI in September 2024. Seven good principles. And the document itself says they are not legally binding.

THE ASYMMETRY

You cannot hold a voluntary instrument to a binding target. That is the fault line.

THESIS

Malaysia does not have an AI ambition problem. It has an AI enforcement problem.

Every growth promise in AI Nation 2030 is binding, dated and measured. Every dignity promise is voluntary, undated and unmeasured. Our proposal does one thing: it makes dignity as measurable as GDP.

THE ARITHMETIC

Say the division out loud

685,000 workers exposed. 22,000 training places funded. That is 3.2 per cent. The reskilling response is a rounding error against the exposure.

THE DIVIDE HAS MOVED

Access is solved. Capability and protection are not.

Almost every Malaysian is online, most of them through a phone. That phone is where deepfake investment scams are delivered. Connectivity without literacy is not inclusion. It is exposure.

THE PRECEDENTS

Malaysia has already legislated harder things

When a judge says our proposals are unrealistic, this is the answer. Five binding Acts in under two years.

THE MOST USEFUL PRECEDENT

A delivery rider can already contest an algorithm

Since 31 March 2026, the Gig Workers Act gives 1.64 million Malaysians the right not to be deactivated by an algorithm without recourse, and a tribunal to hear the complaint. Parliament accepted the principle. We ask for it to be extended to every Malaysian facing a high-stakes automated decision: in health, in credit, in welfare, in school placement.

Why does a loan applicant deserve less protection than a food delivery rider?

LOCK · GOVERN
MMandate it

Binding duties for high-risk public sector AI

THE GAP

AIGE is voluntary. A ministry can deploy an AI system that decides eligibility for aid and comply with nothing.

THE MECHANISM

Classify public sector AI by risk. For the high-risk tier, convert the seven AIGE principles from guidance into statutory duties, using the Cyber Security Act 2024 as the drafting model: named duties, named regulator, named penalty.

LOCK · GOVERN
AAssure it

Nothing high-risk goes live without a MY-AISafe certificate

THE GAP

The assurance body now exists. The Malaysia AI Safety Institute (MY-AISafe) was launched inside AI Malaysia Berhad on 28 July 2026 with a mandate for safety evaluation and red-teaming. It has a mandate. It does not yet have a gate.

THE MECHANISM

One sentence of law: no high-risk public sector AI system goes live without a published MY-AISafe assurance certificate covering bias testing, security red-teaming and a documented failure mode.

LOCK · PROTECT
RReverse it

A statutory right to human review and reversal

THE GAP

A Malaysian denied a loan, a benefit, a school placement or a clinical referral by an automated system has no general right to demand that a human look again.

THE MECHANISM

A right, for any person subject to a high-risk automated decision, to (a) be told a machine was used, (b) receive the reason in plain language and (c) obtain a human review within a fixed statutory window.

LOCK · PROTECT
UUpskill for it

Reskilling funded in proportion to displacement

THE GAP

685,000 workers exposed. 22,000 places funded. 3.2 per cent coverage.

THE MECHANISM

Tie reskilling funding to a published displacement estimate per sector, and levy it where the productivity gain lands. A sector that automates 10,000 roles funds 10,000 transitions. Route it through the existing HRD Corp levy rather than a new tax.

LOCK · PROVE
AAuthenticate it

Provenance before the loss, not takedown after

THE GAP

USD 502 million lost, 99.7 per cent to deepfaked investment offers. The Online Safety Act makes platforms take fraud down after it appears. Nothing makes authentic content provable before it spreads.

THE MECHANISM

Two duties. First, machine-readable provenance on AI-generated media published by any licensed platform or government channel. Second, cryptographic verification of every official government communication channel, so a citizen can check whether a minister actually said it.

LOCK · PROVE
HHold it to account

A dignity dashboard beside the GDP dashboard

What is not measured is not defended. AI Malaysia Berhad already monitors progress across ministries. We add one page to a report it must already produce.

NONE OF THIS IS NEW

We are asking the government to finish what it started

Gig workers already have the right to contest an algorithm (Act 872). Platforms already carry binding duties (Act 866). The plan already names a Trust Function, a classification system and a public trust enabler (E12, E13, E8). The safety institute already exists (MY-AISafe). We are not proposing a fifteenth enabler. We are proposing a deadline for the ones the plan already has.

The workforce of 2030 is in Form Four today. Every number in this presentation lands on our year group, not on the people who wrote the plan.
CLOSE

How we would know whether we stayed ourselves

Every quarter, AI Malaysia will publish how much AI added to our economy. We are asking for one more page in that same report. How many Malaysians asked for a human to look again. How many of those decisions were overturned. How many workers were retrained against how many were displaced. How much we lost to deepfakes. It costs almost nothing to publish. Which is why the only reason not to publish it is that we would rather not know.

AI Nation 2030 tells us what Malaysia wants to become. MARUAH is how we would know whether we stayed ourselves while becoming it.
